# Task cards

Read each one aloud, or print this page, cut it into cards, and hand them over one at a time. The wording deliberately avoids the words on PAGER's buttons.

---

**T1**
Look at this page for a moment. In your own words, what do you think this app is for, and who is it for?

---

**T2**
You'd like to try this app. Create an account for yourself.

---

**T3**
Write down something that happened today, and how you were feeling about it.

---

**T4**
Find out which milestones your child should be reaching around now.
*(Expecting parent: find out where you are in your pregnancy and what's coming up.)*

---

**T5**
Find a piece of advice that would be useful to you right now.
Would you trust it? Why or why not?

---

**T6**
Imagine you're worried about your baby's sleep and would like to talk to a professional about it. Show me what you would do.

---

**T7**
Imagine your situation has changed: your baby has been born, or you've gone back to work. Update the app so it knows.

---

**T8**
You've changed your mind about what you wrote earlier. Remove it.
