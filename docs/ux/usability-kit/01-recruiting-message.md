## Screener questions (send to people who reply)
1. Are you currently pregnant, or the main caregiver of a child aged 0–3?
   *(Yes → continue · No → thank them, not a fit)*
2. How old is your child, or how many weeks pregnant are you?
3. Are you working, on leave, or at home full-time? Are you raising your child mostly on your own?
4. Have you used an app called PAGER before? *(Yes → not a fit)*
