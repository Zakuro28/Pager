## Scoring (for you, not the participant)
1. **Odd items** (1, 3, 5, 7, 9): score − 1
2. **Even items** (2, 4, 6, 8, 10): 5 − score
3. Add the 10 results and **multiply by 2.5** → a score from 0 to 100.

**Reading the score:** the average across many products is about **68**.
- **Above 80:** excellent
- **68–80:** good
- **Below 68:** needs work
- **Below 50:** serious problems

Report the **average of the 5 participants**, and note any one person who scored very low. Their session notes usually explain why.
